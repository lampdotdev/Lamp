Three things that still make Ann Arbor feel like New York or empty. Do them in this order, putting each into the game as it's done, and keep the frame rate where it is (check it before and after each).

1. Michigan cars and plates: no New York left on the roads.
   - Every car's plate is a Michigan plate (the white plate with "Pure Michigan" or "Great Lakes" lettering, random plate numbers), parked and moving, not New York's yellow plates. Keep the player's "GO BLU".
   - Traffic and parked cars look like what Ann Arbor drives: Subarus, Priuses and other hybrids, Hondas and Toyotas, Ford and Chevy pickups, minivans, a few Teslas, the odd old Volvo wagon; muted colors, plenty of silver, white, gray, dark blue and green. Fewer yellow cabs (Ann Arbor has few taxis), U-M blue maintenance trucks and buses on campus.
   - Rename the camera ids, functions and comments still named for Manhattan (centralPark, fifthAve…) to Ann Arbor's, and look for anything else of New York's that shows (signs, plates, sounds).

2. People downtown, who react.
   - Many more pedestrians downtown and on campus, by time of day: busy on Main, State, South University and the Diag in the afternoon and evening, students walking with backpacks, people at outdoor tables on Main when it's warm, fewer late at night and in the neighborhoods.
   - Crowds that read as Ann Arbor: students, families, runners, dog walkers, cyclists.
   - Pedestrians react to the car: they step back from the curb when a car comes close, jump out of the way and shout when it heads for them on a sidewalk, and scatter if it doesn't stop.
   - Keep them cheap: simple far away, animated up close, and only as many as the frame rate allows.

3. Depth on downtown facades.
   - Downtown's brick buildings get real depth: windows set into the wall with sills and lintels, cornices along the roofline, storefronts with big glass, recessed doors and awnings, the store signs mounted on the facade instead of floating in front of it.
   - Upper floors with lit and dark windows, some with curtains or blinds.
   - The asphalt looks worn rather than noisy: darker in the wheel tracks, patches, manhole covers, lighter where it's old.
   - Do it only up close (a few blocks around the player), so far buildings stay as cheap as now.
