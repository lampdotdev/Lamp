Building an app with Lamp is a conversation. You say what you want, Lamp builds
it and opens it, and you say what to change.

## Build an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new app">
    Click **New App** at the bottom of the app list, or press ⌘N. If you
    described an app in the welcome, Lamp has already started on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Say what the app should do: what you give it, and what you want back. Or
    click one of the starter ideas and change the request to suit you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Press Return. If your request is unclear, Lamp asks up to three short
    questions first, each with likely answers to pick from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it build">
    Lamp names the app, designs its icon, builds it and opens it. While it
    works, the chat shows the plan with each part ticked off as it's done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When Lamp finishes, it tells you the main things it assumed, so you can
correct anything it got wrong.

### Write a good request

A good request sounds like explaining a chore to a helpful coworker.

- **Start small.** One job done well beats five done badly. You can add more
  later.
- **Say what goes in and what comes out.** "I drop in receipts as photos or
  PDFs, and I get a list with the shop, the date and the total."
- **Say where it lives.** "A small window I keep open" or "an icon in the menu
  bar at the top of the screen".
- **Mention what matters to you.** "Never move a file without asking me first."

<Tip>
  Too vague: "An app for my sales stuff."

  Better: "An app where I paste the notes from a sales call, and it gives me a
  short summary, the next steps, and a follow-up email I can copy."
</Tip>

## Find your apps

Your apps are listed in Lamp's sidebar. Double-click one to open it.

They are also installed on your Mac like any other app, in **Applications →
Lamp Apps** in your home folder, so you can find them with Spotlight and
Launchpad.

When you're using an app you built and want it to work differently, choose
**Edit with Lamp…** (⇧⌘E) from the app's own menu. It takes you back to that
app's chat in Lamp.

## Ask for a change

Select the app in the sidebar and describe the change in its chat. Be specific
about what happens now and what you want instead:

> The list shows the newest receipt at the bottom. Put the newest at the top,
> and add a total for each month.

Lamp rebuilds the app and reopens it. Anything you saved in the app is kept.

### Change a message you already sent

Click the pencil beside one of your messages. It is always there on your
latest message, and appears on earlier ones when you point at them. Change
the words in the message box, then send as usual.

Lamp returns the app to how it was before that message and makes your new
request instead. The versions made in between stay in **Versions**. If you
change your mind before sending, press Escape to put the chat back as it was.

### Show what you mean

A picture often says it faster. Attach screenshots, documents or data with the
paperclip, drag them onto the chat, or paste a copied image or file. Say what
Lamp should use from each one.

### While Lamp is working

- **Keep typing.** Messages you send wait until the current change is finished,
  then go out together. Click the ✕ beside a waiting message to take it back.
- **Stop.** Click **Stop**, beside the Send button, to cancel the change in
  progress.
- **See more.** Turn on **Agent Details** in the toolbar to see each step Lamp
  takes.
- **Do something else.** If you switch to another app, Lamp sends a
  notification when your app is ready or needs you.

### When Lamp asks permission

Sometimes a request appears above the message box, asking to do something that
could affect more than the app it's building. Read it, then choose **Allow**
or **Deny**. Nothing happens until you answer. See
[Privacy](/sharing-privacy-troubleshooting#privacy) for what Lamp asks about.

## Undo and versions

You can't break an app by asking for the wrong thing. Lamp keeps every version.

- **Undo a change.** Click **Undo Last Change** in the toolbar to go back to
  the version before the last change. Click it again to step further back.
- **Keep a version you like.** Choose **File → Name This Version…** (⌘S) and
  give it a name you'll recognise later.
- **Go back further.** Click **Versions** in the toolbar to see earlier
  versions and return to one. You can also point at one of your requests in
  the chat and click the clock beside it.

Going back changes how the app works and keeps the data you saved in it. Later
versions are kept too, so you can undo going back.

## Open and quit an app

Click **Open App** (the Play button) in the toolbar. While the app is open,
that button becomes **Quit App**. This is the easiest way to quit an app that
lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon.

You can also right-click an app in the sidebar and choose **Open** or **Quit**.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| ⌘N | New App |
| Return | Send a message |
| Shift-Return | Add a new line |
| ⌘V | Paste text, or attach a copied image or file |
| Tab | Use the suggested next request |
| Escape | Put back a message you took from the chat to change |
| ⌘S | Name This Version |
| ⌘, | Settings |
| ⌘/ | Lamp Help |
| ⇧⌘E | Edit with Lamp (in an app you built) |

Lamp also has a guide built in, which works without the internet: choose
**Help → Lamp Help**.
