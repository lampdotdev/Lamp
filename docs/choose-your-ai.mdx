Lamp doesn't come with an AI of its own. It uses one you already have, or one
you sign up for. The welcome asks you to choose, and you can change your mind
at any time.

## The three choices

| Choice | What it uses | Good if |
|---|---|---|
| **Claude Code** | Your Claude subscription | You already pay for Claude |
| **ChatGPT** | Your ChatGPT subscription | You already pay for ChatGPT |
| **Lamp Agent** | An AI you choose: OpenRouter, another provider, or a model on this Mac | You have neither, or you want to pick the AI yourself |

If you already pay for Claude or ChatGPT, start there. It's the quickest way
to get going, and there's nothing new to pay for.

## Claude Code

Uses your Claude subscription.

1. Choose **Claude Code** in the welcome.
2. If Lamp says Claude isn't on this Mac yet, click **Get Claude**, install
   the Claude app, open it once, then come back to Lamp.
3. Click **Use Claude Code**. If you aren't signed in, your browser opens so
   you can sign in with your Claude account. Then come back to Lamp.

## ChatGPT

Uses your ChatGPT subscription.

1. Choose **ChatGPT** in the welcome.
2. If Lamp says ChatGPT isn't on this Mac yet, click **Get ChatGPT**, install
   the ChatGPT app, open it once, then come back to Lamp.
3. Click **Use ChatGPT**. If you aren't signed in, your browser opens so you
   can sign in with your ChatGPT account. Then come back to Lamp.

<Note>
  Outside the welcome, Lamp calls this choice **Codex**. That's the name of
  the part of ChatGPT that writes software. You'll see it in Settings.
</Note>

## Lamp Agent

Lamp's own agent works with the AI you pick. There are three ways to give it
one.

### OpenRouter

OpenRouter is a service that gives you hundreds of AI models through one
account. You add some credit and pay only for what you use. You get a **key**
from it, which is a long password that lets Lamp use your account.

1. Go to [openrouter.ai/keys](https://openrouter.ai/keys), create an account
   and add some credit.
2. Create a key and copy it.
3. In Lamp, choose **Lamp Agent**, then **OpenRouter**, and paste the key.

Lamp checks the key before saving it. If the key was mistyped, or the account
has no credit, Lamp tells you straight away.

### Another provider

If you already have a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or one of about
twenty other AI companies, choose **Another provider**, pick the company and
paste your key.

### A model on this Mac

Lamp can download an AI model and run it on your Mac. It's free and private,
because the AI does its work on your Mac. Lamp recommends a model that fits
your Mac's memory. The download is several gigabytes.

<Warning>
  Models that run on your Mac are slower and less capable than Claude or
  ChatGPT. They make simpler apps, and they sometimes get stuck. For your first
  apps, one of the other choices will give you a much better idea of what Lamp
  can do.
</Warning>

## What it costs

Lamp doesn't charge you for building apps. What you pay depends on your choice:

- **Claude Code and ChatGPT** count against the subscription you already have.
  A lot of building in one day can reach your plan's limit, the same as heavy
  use of Claude or ChatGPT themselves.
- **OpenRouter and other providers** charge your account for what you use.
  Check your balance on the provider's website.
- **A model on this Mac** is free.

## Change it later

Open **Lamp → Settings** (⌘,).

- **Building** chooses what builds your apps, and which model it uses.
- **Providers** is where you add, replace and remove keys, and download models
  for this Mac.
- **Apps** chooses the AI your apps use while they run.

## AI inside your apps

Many apps you build use AI themselves, for example to read a receipt or
summarise a document. They reach it through Lamp, so you never put a key into
an app.

Without an OpenRouter key, the text AI in your apps goes through whatever
builds your apps. Apps that make pictures use a picture model downloaded in
**Settings → Providers → Native Mac AI**, if you have one. Otherwise they use ChatGPT if
you're signed in to it; with neither, an app that wants a picture asks you
for a key.

Apps open Lamp in the background when they need it. You don't have to keep
Lamp's window open.
