## Sharing apps

### Send an app

Click **Share App** in the toolbar, or right-click the app in the sidebar.

- **Share…** sends it with AirDrop, Messages or Mail.
- **Copy App File** (⇧⌘C) puts it on the clipboard, ready to paste into a
  message.
- **Export…** saves it as a file.

The person you send it to needs Lamp to open it. They get their own copy to
use and change. Nothing they do affects yours.

### What is included

| Goes with the app | Stays on your Mac |
|---|---|
| How the app works | Your chat with Lamp |
| Its description and icon | Your keys and sign-ins |
| Attached files the app itself uses, such as a logo | The data you saved in the app |
| | Its earlier versions |
| | Files you attached only to show Lamp something |

### Open an app someone sent you

Double-click the file (its name ends in **.lampapp**), or choose **File →
Import Shared App…** (⌘O).

Before building anything, Lamp shows you a review: a list of what the app can
access. When AI is set up and its check succeeds, it also describes what the
app does, raises any concerns and rates the risk. Read it, then click
**Build & Open** or **Cancel**.

<Warning>
  Lamp's review is a guide, not a guarantee. Only open apps from people you
  trust.
</Warning>

## Privacy

### What goes to the AI

To build and change an app, Lamp sends the AI you chose:

- what you type in the chat
- the files you attach
- the app Lamp is building
- pictures of the app's windows, and what the app reports while it runs, so
  the AI can find and fix problems

The builder sends this to the company behind the AI you picked in
[Choose your AI](/choose-your-ai), under your account with them. A model on
this Mac does the building locally.

Lamp also uses AI to name your app, design its icon and suggest your next
message. If you have an OpenRouter key saved, these text helpers use cloud
models through OpenRouter, even when the builder is a model on your Mac.
Your first request goes into naming and icon design, and recent chat messages
go into follow-up suggestions.

To have these text helpers use your local builder, remove the OpenRouter key:
open **Settings → Providers**, select **OpenRouter**, click the minus button
and confirm **Remove**. This doesn't make every AI feature local; pictures
and AI inside your apps have their own choices. See
[AI inside your apps](/choose-your-ai#ai-inside-your-apps).

To stop sending pictures of windows and what apps report, open **Lamp →
Settings** (⌘,) **→ Building** and turn off the two switches there. That's
worth doing for apps that show things you would rather keep to yourself.

### What stays on your Mac

- Your apps, and the data you save in them.
- Your keys. Lamp keeps them in your Mac's Keychain. Apps you build never see
  them.
- Your chats with Lamp, apart from what is sent to the AI as described above.

### What Lamp asks before doing

While building, the AI works inside the app's own folder. Two things need your
say-so:

- **Running a command on your Mac.** Lamp asks first when a command looks
  risky. In **Settings → Building** you can make it ask more often (**Anything
  unusual** or **Always**) or **Never**.
- **Opening files outside the app's folder.** All three agents ask before
  reading or changing your files outside the app's folder. System files,
  app-building tools and temporary folders are exceptions. You can turn this
  check off in **Settings → Building**. Lamp's keys, sign-ins and your other
  Lamp apps stay off limits.

A request appears above the message box, saying in plain words what the AI
wants to do. Choose **Allow** or **Deny**. Lower-risk requests also offer
**Allow All This Turn**. If you aren't sure, choose **Deny** and ask Lamp in
the chat why it needs it.

### Apps that use your other apps

Lamp can let the apps you build read and use your other apps, such as Mail or
Slack. This needs an OpenRouter key and is off until you turn on **Let apps use your
other apps** in **Settings → Apps**. Lamp installs a helper and guides you
through the permissions it needs in System Settings.

Reading needs no further approval. Each click, keystroke or key press waits
for your OK, unless you choose **Allow All for This Task** for that task.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An app didn't build, or a change failed">
    Read the message in the chat. Lamp tries to fix a failed build by itself,
    up to three times. If it still fails, describe what you wanted and ask Lamp
    to fix it, or click **Undo Last Change**. **Agent Details** in the toolbar
    shows more of what happened.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The app opens, but it doesn't do what I asked">
    Tell Lamp in the app's chat. Say what you did, what happened and what you
    expected. A screenshot helps: paste it into the chat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My key or sign-in doesn't work">
    For a key, open **Settings → Providers**, double-click the provider and
    replace its key. Check on the provider's website that your account has
    credit left.

    For Claude Code or ChatGPT, **Settings → Building** shows whether it is
    installed and signed in, with a **Sign In…** button if it isn't.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Apple's app-building tools need attention">
    After a macOS update, the tools can go missing. Lamp shows its welcome
    again and opens Apple's installer. Click **Install**, then **Agree**. If
    the installer doesn't appear, click **Show Installer Again** in Lamp.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An app says it couldn't reach Lamp">
    Open Lamp and try again. If the app still can't reach Lamp, quit any extra
    copies of Lamp and reopen the one you want to use.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Edit with Lamp opens the wrong copy of Lamp">
    You have more than one Lamp on this Mac. Open the copy you want once, or
    move the copies you no longer use to the Trash.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A model on this Mac won't start">
    Try a smaller model. It may need more memory than your Mac has.
    Double-click **Native Mac AI** in **Settings → Providers** to see how much
    memory each model needs and to download another.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An app has an orange badge in the sidebar">
    The app was built by an older version of Lamp. Click **Rebuild** in the
    banner above the message box to bring it up to date. Its data is kept.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
