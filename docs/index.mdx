Describe what you need, and Lamp builds a real Mac app for it. You can keep
using the app, change it whenever you like by saying what you want, and send it
to someone else.

You don't write any code, and you don't need to know how apps are made. You
type what you want in plain words, the way you would explain it to a person.

## What people build with it

- **Downloads Tidy** groups everything in your Downloads folder by what it is
  (receipts, installers, screenshots), suggests clear names, and only moves
  files once you approve.
- **Screenshot Notes** takes the screenshots you drop on it and pulls out the
  text and key details (who, what, dates, amounts) into a list you can search.
- **Reading Queue** takes PDFs and links, asks what you're working on, and ranks
  them by what matters, with a one-line summary and a reason for each.

Start with one small, annoying task. Then keep improving the app until it works
exactly your way.

## What you need

- A Mac with Apple Silicon (an M1 chip or later), running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.
- An internet connection.
- An AI to do the building. A Claude or ChatGPT subscription works, and so does
  a pay-as-you-go key. [Choose your AI](/choose-your-ai) explains the options.

Setting up takes about 15 minutes, most of it waiting for a download from
Apple. Your first app is usually ready a minute or two after that.
