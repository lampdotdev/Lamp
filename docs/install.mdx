Installing Lamp takes a couple of minutes. The first time you open it, Lamp
walks you through a short welcome and gets two things ready before it builds
your first app.

## Before you start

You need a Mac running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. To check, open the Apple
menu in the top-left corner of your screen and choose **About This Mac**.

It also helps to decide what will build your apps. If you already pay for
Claude or ChatGPT, you can use that. See [Choose your AI](/choose-your-ai).

## Install it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Lamp">
    Open the download link in your invitation. A file whose name starts with
    **Lamp** and ends in **.dmg** lands in your Downloads folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the download">
    Double-click the file. A window opens showing Lamp, an arrow and the
    Applications folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag Lamp to Applications">
    Drag the Lamp icon onto the Applications folder in that window. When the
    copy finishes, you can close the window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Lamp">
    Open your Applications folder and double-click **Lamp**. The first time,
    macOS says Lamp was downloaded from the internet and asks whether you want
    to open it. Click **Open**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The welcome

The first time you open Lamp, it takes you through three screens.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get Started">
    Lamp asks "What do you wish you had?" Click **Get Started**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what your first app should do">
    Type what you'd like, starting with "An app that…". You can also click the
    microphone and describe it out loud, or pick one of the ideas under
    **Need an idea?** and change it to suit you. Then click **Continue**.

    Not sure yet? Click **Skip for now**. You can describe an app later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Lamp ready">
    Lamp needs two things, and they can happen at the same time:

    - **Apple's app-building tools.** These are free from Apple, and you don't
      need any special account. If they're missing, Apple's installer window
      opens. Click **Install**, then **Agree**. The download takes 5–10
      minutes, and Lamp carries on by itself when it's done.
    - **Your AI agent.** Pick Claude Code, ChatGPT or Lamp Agent while the
      tools install. [Choose your AI](/choose-your-ai) explains each one.

    When both show a tick, click **Start building**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you described an app in the welcome, Lamp starts building it straight away.
[Build and change your first app](/first-app) covers what happens next.

<Tip>
  If Apple's installer window doesn't appear, or you closed it by accident,
  click **Show Installer** in Lamp to bring it back.
</Tip>

## Keeping Lamp up to date

Lamp checks for a newer version once a day. When there is one, it shows you
what changed, with an **Install** button. To check yourself, choose **Lamp →
Check for Updates…** from the menu bar.

Your apps stay where they are when Lamp updates.
