# Real places from map data

A real place (a town to drive, a campus, a neighborhood) comes from OpenStreetMap. Fetch it from the Overpass API (overpass-api.de, else a mirror), one bounding-box query for everything in the box and what it's made of (`nwr(…); (._; >;); out;`: nodes too, since trees, shops and signals are often single points), waiting and trying again when it's busy; never OpenStreetMap's own editing API, which its rules keep from bulk downloads. Turn it, while building, into a compact file in Assets/ that the app reads (meters from a center point, footprints already triangulated), not raw OSM parsed at launch. Worth reading: each building's footprint, `height` or else `building:levels` (about 3.3 m a floor) or else a guess from `building` (house, garage, apartments, university…), `roof:shape`, `building:color`, `roof:color` and `building:material` (use them when they're there, and choose from the building's type only when they aren't), and `building:part` for landmarks' stepped parts; roads with `highway`, `width` or `lanes`, `oneway` and `name`; traffic signals, crossings, footpaths and trees; the ground: parks, gardens, pitches, grass, cemeteries (`leisure`, `landuse`), water and rivers (`natural=water`, `waterway`), woods (`natural=wood`, `landuse=forest`), car parks; shops and amenities with `name`, `opening_hours` and address. Large areas and buildings with courtyards are often relations (`type=multipolygon`), not closed ways: join their `outer` members' ways end to end into rings and cut out the `inner` ones, or the biggest parks, the river and many landmarks go missing. An area larger than a tile (a park, a river, a lake) is cut along the tile edges while building the map file, each piece clipped to its square and drawn by that tile, so it shows wherever the player is in it, not only near its center. In a 3D scene, stream the world by tile (the scene-3d skill's `scene.stream`), and show "© OpenStreetMap contributors" on screen.
