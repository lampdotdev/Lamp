# What makes the game good to play

The web-game guide says how the game runs on Lamp's runtime; this says what makes it worth playing, and every game
needs it. Where the two touch the runtime (files, input, how sound loads), the guide holds. A section below that points
to the game or game-ui skill means the part that's here; open another skill only when the guide says the game needs it.

{{sections}}

## One critique, at the end

Once the guide's checks pass, look once more at the opening, a moment of play and the results, in the pictures the
checks already gave you, against what the person asked for. Name at most one thing a player would trip on or that reads
as a template, and fix it, or say nothing is worth changing. A screen that works isn't restyled or redrawn for this,
and it takes no new round of looks beyond rechecking what you changed. A control or a step of the flow that doesn't
work is fixed however many passes it takes.
