Fill the town with more of what the map already knows about. Most of it is already downloaded and unused: OpenStreetMap in .lamp/reference/aa/osm.json and poi/osm_pois.json, and the city's Park Amenities layer in .lamp/reference/aa/aa_parkamenities.geojson (`FeatureTyp`). Build each kind the way the bike racks and street furniture are built (a small builder in Assets/Tools writing a data file in Web/data, drawn by its own module, instanced, set on the ground's height, near ones in full and far ones simple or left out). Put each into the game as it's done, credit every source in credits.json, and keep APP.md's Data section up to date. Check the frame rate and memory before and after each step: the game already runs close to the browser's memory limit on lamp.dev, so nothing here may add much.

1. Swimming pools (OSM `leisure=swimming_pool`, about 130, nearly all backyard pools, plus the public pools at Fuller, Buhr and Veterans Memorial): sunk into the ground with tiled walls and coping, clear blue water in the game's water shading, a ladder or steps, and for the public ones lane lines, a diving board, lifeguard chairs and a fence. Swimmable: the player swims in them the way they swim in the river (the same `swimming` on foot), with splashes going in; a car or bike driven in sinks to the bottom and the camera goes under, as in the river. Backyard ones get loungers and maybe a float.
2. Outdoor seating (OSM `leisure=outdoor_seating`, about 66, and restaurants and cafes with `outdoor_seating=yes`): patio tables, chairs and umbrellas on the sidewalk or patio in front of the place, people sitting and eating at some of them in daytime and warm weather, put away in winter and at night. Tables and chairs go flying when hit like benches do.
3. Ball diamonds (OSM `leisure=pitch` with `sport=baseball` or `softball`, and the city's Ball Diamond and Team Bench amenities): an infield of red-brown clay with the base paths, bases, home plate and pitcher's mound, chalk lines, a backstop, dugouts or team benches, and an outfield fence where mapped. Bleachers (OSM `leisure=bleachers`, and the city's Sports Viewing) at the fields and tracks: aluminum rows on a frame that the player can climb and sit on.
4. Benches and the rest of the street furniture: add OSM's street-side `amenity=bench`, `waste_basket`, `post_box`, `vending_machine`, `bicycle_repair_station` and `drinking_water` (fetch these by area as fetch-annarbor.py does the rest, since osm.json doesn't hold most of them), alongside the park ones already drawn and never doubled with them. Benches you can sit on, the rest knocked over or thrown like the trash cans are.
5. Boat launches, fishing and boat docks, with boats (OSM `leisure=slipway`, `man_made=pier`, the city's Boat Dock, Fishing Dock and Canoe Livery): a concrete ramp into the water at each launch with a pickup and empty trailer parked nearby; wooden docks the player can walk out on (like Gallup's, `dockTop`), with canoes, kayaks, rowboats or small motorboats tied up, and someone fishing off the end now and then. A boat at a dock can be taken out the way the kayaks on the banks are.
6. The skate park (Ann Arbor Skatepark at Veterans Memorial Park; check its outline in OSM) and mountain bike trails (the city's Mountain Bike amenities, and OSM `mtb:scale` or `mtb=yes` paths at Bird Hills, Olson and the rest): the skate park a concrete bowl, ramps, rails and ledges that the bike and car ride on the way they ride the stadium's stands, with a few skaters; the trails narrow dirt singletrack through the woods, with roots, berms and little jumps, and a trailhead sign at each end.
7. The bandshell (the city's Bandshell, at West Park): its curved shell and stage over the lawn's terraced seating, with a band playing there on summer evenings, heard from across the park. Restrooms and pit toilets (the city's Restrooms and Pit Toilet amenities): small park restroom buildings and the odd portable toilet, a door that opens.

8. Parking structures (OSM `amenity=parking` with `parking=multi-storey`, about 30: the city's Ann Ashley, Fourth & Washington, Fourth & William, Maynard, Liberty Square, Forest and the rest, and U-M's, matched to their surveyed buildings, with `building:levels` where mapped, else from the survey height at about 3 m a deck). Today they're drawn as ordinary buildings; make them what they are: open concrete decks on columns with low parapet walls and the gaps between showing the cars inside, ramps between decks, stair and elevator towers, an entrance with a gate arm and a "P" sign on the street it opens to (from OSM's entrance where mapped), lights under each deck at night. Drivable: the car, bikes and the player on foot go in through the entrance, up the ramps deck by deck and out onto the open roof (each deck a surface the way the stadium's stands are, `carSurface`; the columns and parapets solid), the gate arm snapping off if driven through. Parked cars fill most stalls on every deck (the same parked cars, streamed in only near the player), and the roof is the best view downtown. A car can jump off the roof the way it jumps anywhere else.

Things you can sit on (benches, bleachers, patio chairs) work the same way: on foot, the get-in key beside one sits the player down facing out, and moving gets up.
