Lemon chickpea skillet

A warm pan, a handful of greens, and dinner that asks very little of you.

Some evenings, I want dinner to be simple. This is the dish I come back to when there are chickpeas in the cupboard and half a lemon in the fridge. It is generous, bright, and at home beside a slice of good bread.

Before we get to the recipe, a note about the pan: use whatever you like cooking with. A wide skillet gives the chickpeas room. This little supper is just as good eaten straight from a bowl on the sofa.

The recipe

20 minutes · Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 can (400 g) chickpeas, drained
  • 100 g baby spinach
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • 2 tablespoons plain yogurt
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Warm the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  2. Add the drained chickpeas and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  3. Fold in the spinach and cook for 2 minutes until wilted.
  4. Stir in the lemon zest and juice. Season with salt and black pepper.
  5. Divide between two bowls and finish each with a spoonful of yogurt.
